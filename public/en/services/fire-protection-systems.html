Fire Protection Systems

Automatic sprinklers, wet and dry suppression, fire pump sets, fire detection and voice alarm.

We engineer fire suppression, detection and alarm systems to national and international standards, protecting people and assets in buildings and industrial facilities.

Scope of service

  • Fire suppression pipework
  • Wet and dry fire suppression systems
  • Automatic sprinkler systems
  • Fire pump sets
  • Fire detection systems
  • Voice alarm and evacuation systems

Our approach

We design and install complete fire safety systems, from sprinkler and hydrant mains to fire pump rooms, detection and voice alarm, matched to each building's hazard classification and current regulations. Our experience covers wet, dry and sprinkler systems in factories, warehouses, hotels, schools and offices.

Frequently asked questions

What types of building do you work on?

We work on projects of every scale, from homes and villas to hotels, offices, schools, healthcare buildings, factories and warehouses. Recent projects in this field include Robert College, Özyeğin University, Silivri Factory.

Do you handle both design and installation?

Yes. We can act as your single point of contact for the whole process, from survey and design through procurement, installation, testing and commissioning, or support you at the design, site supervision or installation stage only.

Which regions do you cover?

We are based in Maltepe, Istanbul, and work across Türkiye, mainly in Istanbul and Kocaeli, with completed projects in Kırklareli, Çorum, Nevşehir and Muğla.

What information do you need for a quote?

Just send us the project type, approximate floor area (m²), location and the services you need, via the contact form, phone or email. Where needed, we will arrange a site survey before preparing our technical assessment and quotation.
